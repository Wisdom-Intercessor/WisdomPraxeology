# WisdomPraxeology — Initial Requirements

**Version:** 0.1.0-proposal

- **MUST:** every action/decision records goal, context, accountable owner, authority, constraints and completion criteria.
- **MUST:** link decisions to relevant evidence, uncertainty and value criteria.
- **MUST:** separate deliberation, authorization, plan, execution and outcome states.
- **MUST:** high-impact actions include human approval, risk controls, stop conditions and rollback where feasible.
- **MUST:** capture outcomes and unintended effects for review and learning.
- **MUST:** preserve action/decision history and change rationale.
- **MUST:** maintain root interfaces and traceability to consuming operational repositories.
- **SHOULD:** provide bilingual vocabulary and structured action/decision records.
- **SHOULD:** validate schemas, links and lifecycle states automatically.

Requirements become accepted only with review and verification evidence. Documentation is not proof of implemented workflow or production readiness.
