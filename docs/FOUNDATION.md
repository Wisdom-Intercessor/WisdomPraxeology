# WisdomPraxeology — Foundational Charter

**Document ID:** WI-FP-001 | **Version:** 0.1.0-proposal | **Status:** Draft for review

## Purpose
Define a disciplined framework for converting understanding and values into purposeful deliberation, decisions, plans, action, execution, observation, feedback and improvement. Praxeology here is a project-specific action framework, not a claim to settle any academic school.

## Scope
Problem framing; goals and constraints; options; deliberation and decisions; planning; coordination; execution; monitoring; outcomes; unintended effects; learning and corrective action.

## Boundaries
Ontology defines canonical action-related entities and concepts; Epistemology evaluates supporting claims, evidence and uncertainty; Axiology supplies normative values, rights and criteria; Teleology supplies purpose hierarchy, ultimate ends and long-range direction. This root integrates these inputs into accountable operational action but does not own their definitions, epistemic judgments, value standards or ultimate ends.

## Principles
1. Actions have explicit goals, scope, owner, authority, constraints and completion criteria.
2. Decisions link to evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and value criteria.
3. Distinguish deliberation, authorization, plan, execution and outcome.
4. Material-risk actions require proportionate safeguards, human approval and stop/rollback plans.
5. Measure outcomes and unintended consequences; learn from failures without erasing history.
6. Respect consent, rights, privacy and delegated-authority limits.
7. Plans are adaptable; changes record rationale and author.

## Vocabulary
Problem; need; goal; objective; option; deliberation; decision; authority; plan; action; workflow; resource; constraint; execution; observation; output; outcome; impact; feedback; correction; learning.

## Responsibilities
Maintain the action/decision framework, lifecycle and controls; define cross-layer interfaces; advise operational implementation; preserve accountable human decision ownership.

## Acceptance
This charter is proposed. Operational use requires contextual risk assessment, approval and evidence; this document alone does not establish deployed capability.
